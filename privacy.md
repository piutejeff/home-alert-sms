# Privacy Policy

This is a private home-monitoring SMS notification service operated for personal use.

The service uses the mobile phone number of the system owner solely to deliver automated home-monitoring and safety notifications. These may include water leak alerts, temperature alerts, equipment status alerts, and other configured home notifications.

Mobile information, opt-in data, and consent will not be shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Message frequency varies depending on home-monitoring events and system configuration. Message and data rates may apply.

The service is not used for marketing or promotional messages.
