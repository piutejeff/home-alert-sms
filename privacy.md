# Privacy Policy

## Home Alert SMS

Home Alert SMS is a private home-monitoring notification service operated by the system owner for personal use.

### Information Collected

The service collects and uses the mobile phone number of the system owner for the sole purpose of delivering automated SMS home-monitoring and safety notifications.

These notifications may include water leak alerts, temperature alerts, equipment status alerts, and other notifications generated by the owner's Home Assistant home-automation system.

### Use of Mobile Information

Mobile phone numbers and SMS messaging consent information are used only to provide the Home Alert SMS notification service.

We do not share, sell, rent, or provide mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

### SMS Messaging

The system owner explicitly opts in by manually configuring their own mobile telephone number in their private Home Assistant system for the purpose of receiving Home Alert SMS notifications.

There is no public enrollment or signup process. The system owner is the sole intended recipient.

Message frequency varies according to home-monitoring events and system configuration. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages. Reply HELP for assistance.

### Marketing

Home Alert SMS does not send advertising, marketing, promotional, affiliate, or third-party messages.
