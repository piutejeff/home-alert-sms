# Terms and Conditions

## Home Alert SMS

Home Alert SMS is a private SMS notification service used by the system owner to receive automated home-monitoring and safety alerts from their Home Assistant system.

### Service Description

Messages may include water leak alerts, temperature alerts, equipment status alerts, and other notifications generated by the owner's private home-automation system.

The system owner is the sole intended recipient. There is no public enrollment, subscription, or marketing program.

### Consent

The system owner consents to receive Home Alert SMS messages by manually entering and configuring their own mobile phone number in their private Home Assistant system.

By configuring the phone number for SMS notifications, the system owner expressly agrees to receive automated home-monitoring and safety text messages.

### Message Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies depending on home-monitoring events and system configuration.

Message and data rates may apply.

### Opt-Out and Help

Reply STOP to opt out of SMS notifications.

Reply HELP for assistance.

After opting out, no further SMS notifications will be sent unless the system owner intentionally re-enables SMS notifications and provides consent again.

### Support

For assistance with Home Alert SMS, contact the service operator at:
https://github.com/piutejeff/home-alert-sms/issues

Reply HELP to the SMS number for messaging assistance.

### Privacy

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent information are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

See the Privacy Policy for additional information.

### Marketing

Home Alert SMS does not send advertising, marketing, promotional, affiliate, or third-party messages.
