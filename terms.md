# Terms and Conditions

This is a private SMS notification service used by the system owner to receive automated home-monitoring and safety alerts.

Messages may include water leak alerts, temperature alerts, equipment status alerts, and other notifications generated by the owner's home automation system.

Message frequency varies depending on detected events and system configuration. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to opt out of SMS notifications. Reply HELP for assistance.

This service does not send marketing or promotional messages.
