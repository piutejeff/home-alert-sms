These Terms and Conditions apply to the SMS messaging program operated by Jeffrey Baker under the registered Jeffrey Baker Sole Proprietor Brand.

Jeffrey Baker Home Monitoring provides automated home-monitoring and safety notifications generated by a private Home Assistant system.

### Service Description

Messages may include water leak alerts, temperature alerts, equipment status alerts, and other notifications generated by the owner's private home-automation system.

The system owner is the sole intended recipient. There is no public enrollment, subscription, or marketing program.

### Consent

SMS messaging is optional and is not enabled automatically.

The system owner opts in by manually entering and configuring their own mobile phone number in their private Home Assistant system and enabling SMS notifications for that number.

By configuring the phone number and enabling SMS notifications, the system owner expressly agrees to receive automated home-monitoring and safety text messages from Jeffrey Baker.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not required to use Home Assistant or any other service.

### Message Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies depending on home-monitoring events and system configuration.

Message and data rates may apply.

Carriers are not liable for any delayed or undelivered messages.

### Opt-Out and Help

**Reply STOP to opt out of SMS notifications.**

**Reply HELP for assistance.**

After opting out, no further SMS notifications will be sent unless the system owner intentionally re-enables SMS notifications and provides consent again.
